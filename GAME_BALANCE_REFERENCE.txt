11. ENEMY WAVE PROGRESSION & DYNAMIC SCALING FORMULAS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every round (wave), monster attributes dynamically scale according to the following exact formulas:
  - Standard Monster HP:     HP  = Base_HP  * (1.0 + (wave - 1) * 0.16) * Difficulty_Enemy_HP_Mult
  - Monster Contact Damage:  DMG = Base_DMG * (1.0 + (wave - 1) * 0.07) * Difficulty_Enemy_DMG_Mult
  - Monster Movement Speed:  SPD = Base_SPD * min(1.35, 1.0 + (wave - 1) * 0.015)
  - Boss Monster HP:         HP  = Base_HP  * (1.0 + (wave - 1) * 0.16) * (1.0 + (wave - 1) * 0.12) * Difficulty_Boss_HP_Mult

Elite & Miniboss Multipliers (Applied when an elite or miniboss spawns):
  - Elite Monster:    HP x2.40  |  Contact DMG x1.30  |  Bone Drops +1  |  Collision Radius x1.20  |  Scale x1.15
  - Miniboss Monster: HP x3.50  |  Contact DMG x1.50  |  Bone Drops +2  |  Collision Radius x1.35  |  Scale x1.35
